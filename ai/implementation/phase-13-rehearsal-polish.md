# Phase 13 - Rehearsal and Polish

## Objective
Make the feature-complete app dependable for weekly sessions.

## Scope
- Phone ergonomics and no-scroll review.
- TV readability at actual viewing distance.
- Loading, retry, connection, and persistence errors.
- Session indicators and destructive-action protection.
- Disconnected phones, reconnects, guests, full roster, and server restart rehearsal.
- Preserve sounds and theme.

## Dependencies
Phases 10-12.

## Deliverables
Usability fixes, recovery behavior, final control layout, and session-readiness checklist.

## Success criteria
DM primarily uses the phone; players find spells and control timers; TV is readable; failure states are visible and recoverable; the workflow is faster than the current app.
